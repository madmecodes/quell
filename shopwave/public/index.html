What is ShopWave?

A live demo store — the application that AppMedic watches.

The point

ShopWave is a normal e-commerce store with real users browsing and checking out. It emits live telemetry (traces, business events) just like a real app.

The Chaos Panel

Click Inject: slow payment to simulate a bad deploy that slows the payment service — but only for Android shoppers in India. Their checkout starts failing and they begin abandoning carts.

What happens next

On the AppMedic console, the AI agents notice the degraded experience, trace it to the exact service and deploy, quantify the revenue at risk, and — with your approval — roll it back. Click Clear fault and AppMedic reports all healthy again.

In short: this is the patient. AppMedic is the doctor.
