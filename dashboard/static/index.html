How AppMedic works

A team of AI agents that catches a bad user experience, traces the cause, and prevents it — with you in control.

What you are looking at

AppMedic watches ShopWave, a live store. When a fault hurts real users, six specialized agents run as a pipeline. You approve the fix and the learning. Everything is powered by Gemini on Google Cloud Agent Builder, using the Dynatrace MCP as the agents' senses and hands.

The six agents

Watcher — spots the degraded user segment from real-user data
Tracer — finds the exact service, span and deploy at fault
Judge — quantifies users, carts and revenue at risk
Actuator — executes the approved rollback and alerts ops
Scribe — writes the prevented-incident report
Evaluator — grades the agents from their own traces

What happens, step by step

1
You inject a fault on ShopWave (slow payments for one segment).
2
Watcher → Tracer → Judge investigate and report what's wrong, why, and the business impact.
3
Gate 1 — you approve the action. Nothing touches production without you.
4
Actuator rolls back the bad deploy; Scribe logs the rescue.
5
Evaluator grades each agent and proposes improvements.
6
Gate 2 — you approve the learning. The lesson is saved to the agent's memory, so next run it's faster and smarter.

Why two approvals

Human in the loop, twice: once before any change to production, once before the system changes itself. Autonomy with oversight.
